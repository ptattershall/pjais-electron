# PJais -- Product Overview

PJais is a **local‑first AI business operating system** that helps
people plan, launch, and maintain a business.\
The application runs as a desktop app built with **Electron** and uses
**local AI models** so users can operate privately and offline.

## Core Idea

PJais organizes a business into **Workspaces**, where each workspace
represents a business project.\
Inside each workspace, the user can:

- Plan their business using **structured phases**
- Track **action items and tasks**
- Store documents and business knowledge
- Capture **structured memories** in a dedicated screen, with an
  **AI-suggested save flow** (review, edit, approve) so only approved
  facts enter long-term memory
- Use AI that remembers only relevant business information
- Maintain operations with recurring business checks

## Key Product Components

### Workspaces

A workspace represents one business. Each workspace contains: phases;
tasks; documents in the vault; **business memories** (managed in-app and
used for AI context); and AI chat scoped to that workspace.

### Phases

Phases guide the user through building a business step‑by‑step.

Example phases: 1. Idea → Offer 2. Brand & Messaging 3. Operations Setup
4. Build & Launch 5. Sales System 6. Marketing System 7. Finance & Admin
8. Maintenance

Each phase generates **action items** that users can complete.

### Action Items

Tasks connected to phases.

Each task may include: - description - owner - due date - completion
notes - attached evidence or documents

### Document Vault

A workspace contains a vault where the user can store: - notes - SOPs -
strategy documents - uploaded files

These documents are indexed for AI retrieval.

### Business memories

Important facts (brand voice, offers, policies, and similar) live as
**structured memory records** in the workspace. Users can manage them on
the **Memories** screen and optionally accept **AI-suggested** entries
from the copilot after review.

### AI Copilot

The AI assistant can: - help plan business strategy - generate marketing
ideas - assist with tasks - summarize documents - suggest improvements

AI only accesses information from the **current workspace**.

### Maintenance Hub

A section dedicated to **running the business long‑term**.

Example modules: - weekly review checklist - KPI tracking - marketing
calendar - customer follow‑ups - operational reminders
